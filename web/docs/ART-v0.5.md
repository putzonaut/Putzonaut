## public/art/station-v5.webp

Transparenter Hintergrund: nein.

Finaler Prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a polished illustration asset for the mobile household game Putzonaut. Landscape 1536 x 1024. A cozy dollhouse cutaway space station resting on a small moon platform in a deep midnight navy starfield. Style: soft rounded toy-like 3D storybook illustration, beautiful painterly materials, warm golden interior lanterns, navy-blue shell, cyan glows, touches of purple, detailed yet very readable at phone size. Whole station fully visible with a little breathing room; no framing cutoffs. Arrange three clearly separated parts around a central golden living capsule: LEFT an enclosed lush green plant nook with leafy houseplants in terracotta pots; LOWER CENTER foreground a small charming round cleaning robot with a navy face and cyan eyes, holding a tiny brush; RIGHT a rounded glass observatory dome with a telescope pointed toward the stars and cyan reflections. The central base has a little orange sofa and warm lit doorway and reads as home. Main modules should have generous visual separation so the app can reveal left, bottom center and right regions independently. Frontal slightly elevated cutaway view, balanced broad silhouette, not a busy city scene. Family friendly, joyful, high-quality game concept art. No people, no birds, no skulls, no lettering, no words, no UI, no logos, no watermark. Avoid photorealism, flat vector art or simple icon style.
